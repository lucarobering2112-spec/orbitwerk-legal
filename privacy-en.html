2. Summary

Nova Merge does not collect, store or transmit personal data to us or to third parties. The app needs no registration, no account and no internet connection. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking services.

3. Data on your device

To keep your progress, the app stores the following only locally on your device: unlocked levels and stars, in-game currency (“credits”) and boosters, the time of your last lucky-wheel spin, language, and sound/music settings. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your system settings. Credits have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real money.

4. Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions (such as location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage).

5. Sharing with third parties

No data is shared with third parties.

6. Children

The app does not collect data from children or any other users.

7. Your rights

Because we do not process personal data, we hold no data about you. If you have questions, contact us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Changes

If we add features in the future (for example ads or in-app purchases), we will update this policy beforehand and point it out in the app.
